NC Brand Sales Deep Dive

+7.5% WoW

w/c 26 Oct vs 2 Nov 2026 — New customer net sales by brand (UK e-com, excl. Healf Zone, HX, POS). NC £425.4k and 6,250 new customers - the most new customers since mid-September and the highest NC sales since late September, despite WHOOP giving back £26.6k. Gifting and devices did it, mostly on higher discount: NC discount rose 12.9% → 16.0% and NC product margin fell 2.1pp to 31.2%.

The trade has flipped. For six weeks after Healf Week we took discount out, gained margin and lost new customers. This week ran the other way: 3.1pp more NC discount, 463 more new customers, 2.1pp less NC margin. Six gifting and device brands that raised discount by 5–16pp - Hatch, Omnilux, Ozlo, Hyperice, HigherDOSE, NodPod - added £37.7k of NC between them. Without WHOOP, NC was +16%. November 1–8: 7,275 new customers, −8% YoY, against −18% for October.
Data filters: New customers only • UK market • Excludes Healf Zone, HX channel, POS • Sale transactions only • Excludes customer draft orders • Healf warehouse, 9 Nov 2026, Sunday 8 Nov loaded to 23:59. Restatement: NC retros for earlier weeks moved in both directions on this pull (w/c 26 Oct £4,865 → £4,492, w/c 12 Oct £6,414 → £5,819). Sales and customer counts unchanged.

Key takeaways

1
6,250 new customers, the most since mid-September - with WHOOP giving back £26.6k. NC +7.5% to £425.4k. Without WHOOP, NC was +16%. 255 brands up, 213 down - the first time in four weeks with more brands up than down.
2
The volume came back with the discount. NC discount 12.9% → 16.0%, NC product margin 33.3% → 31.2%, retro-adjusted 32.4%, the lowest in the window. Six gifting and device brands that raised discount 5–16pp added £37.7k of NC - more than the week's whole gain. The trade that ran one way for six weeks has flipped.
3
Hatch is the #1 NC brand. £9.0k → £22.5k, units 87 → 262, on discount 6% → 18%. Omnilux £4.4k → £15.4k on 11% → 23%. Lola (+139%) and Therabody (+96%) hit window highs without extra discount - those are the cleaner Q4 signals.
4
November 1–8 is −8% YoY on new customers, against −18% for October. 7,275 vs 7,920. NC sales are still −13% YoY on lower spend per customer. The LE9 36k target assumed flat YoY, with Black Friday still ahead.
5
Retro off, sales down, for the third time. SURI −65% NC the week its cover ended, after Withings and Hunter & Gather. Withings is now at a window low, under half its level of three weeks ago. Oura is the exception, holding NC with no cover.
6
Wearables outside the devices push are still soft. WHOOP back to £20.3k, its normal range. COROS fourth fall, window low. Garmin halved despite discount rising to 16%.
7
DFH NC at a window high, on doubled discount. £3.7k → £4.9k (+34%), with discount 9.8% → 19.0%. Relevant to the DFH push: the lift this week was priced in.
