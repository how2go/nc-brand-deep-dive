NC Brand Sales Deep Dive

+21.1% WoW

w/c 2 Nov vs 9 Nov 2026 — New customer net sales by brand (UK e-com, excl. Healf Zone, HX, POS). NC £515.2k and 7,173 new customers - the most since the end of August. Gifting and devices led again: Omnilux is the #1 NC brand at £38.0k. NC product margin fell to 25.6% while the recorded discount rate fell to 11.6% - the November sale prices are set as live prices, so the discount rate no longer shows the promotion.

Read margin, not discount, from here to Black Friday. NC discount fell 16.0% → 11.6% but NC product margin fell 31.2% → 25.6%. Products are tagged for November sales with a reduced live price against a was-price, so the cut does not appear as a discount. Brand retros went £4.9k → £24.2k on NC and cover part of it: NC retro-adjusted margin is 30.4%, down 2.0pp. November 1–15: 14,447 new customers, −4% YoY, against −8% for 1–8 Nov and −18% for October.

Key takeaways

1
7,173 new customers, the most since the end of August. NC +21% to £515.2k. Four weeks: new customers +30%, NC sales +37%. November 1–15 is −4% YoY on new customers, narrower than September (−5%) or October (−18%).
2
Read margin, not discount, from here to Black Friday. Discount fell to 11.6% but NC product margin fell 5.6pp to 25.6%, the lowest in the window. November sale prices sit in the live price, so the discount rate misses them. Retro-adjusted NC margin is 30.4%, down 2.0pp.
3
Omnilux and Hatch are flying without brand funding. Omnilux £38.0k NC (#1 brand), Hatch £33.9k. Hatch three consecutive rises, Omnilux two; no retro; over 80% new customers. Protect stock on both through Black Friday.
4
Bon Charge flew on cover. NC £1.3k → £11.7k, units 50 → 439, behind a 30% offer on 11 Nov and £27.1k of retro across all customers. See the EC report for the return on that cover.
5
Our Place bounced once stock landed; Lumie hasn't moved. Our Place units 8 → 86, NC £1.4k → £4.4k - it was stock-bound, not demand-bound. Lumie is at £2.7k NC on ~40 units with less discount - that looks like demand or visibility. Nurosym rose 60% on new cover but is still only £2.2k NC.
6
NodPod doubled when its Black Friday stock landed. £7.4k → £14.5k NC, units 318 → 661, after the 3,300-unit order arrived around 10 Nov. Lola is flat with 6 of 8 variants on pre-order; Bearaby picked up the weighted-blanket demand (+182%).
7
Supplements are losing new customers to gifting. Momentous (window low, 25 variants on pre-order), DFH and Kiki down on NC while their all-customer sales held or grew. Codeage fell on both.
