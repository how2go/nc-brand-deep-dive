EC Brand Sales Deep Dive

+12.5% WoW

w/c 2 Nov vs 9 Nov 2026 — Total e-commerce net sales, all customers (UK, excl. Healf Zone, HX, POS). £1.74m, the highest week since at least mid-September. New customers +21%, existing +9%, AOV a window-high £60.36. Product margin fell 4.7pp to 31.2%; brand retros went £20k → £73k and hold retro-adjusted margin at 35.5%. 301 of 563 brands up.

The November sale started, and it is brand-funded. 24 brands carried more than £400 of retro this week, against one last week. Sale prices are set as live prices against a was-price, so the discount rate fell (13.6% → 10.6%) while product margin fell 4.7pp - read margin, not discount. Bon Charge (+£48.6k on £27.1k of cover), Omnilux (+£28.9k) and Hatch (+£13.3k) drove the gain. November 1–15: £3.56m, +9.6% YoY.

Key takeaways

1
£1.74m, +12.5%, the highest week since at least mid-September. Every day up on last week. New customers +21%, existing +9%, AOV £60.36. Four weeks: sales +24%, new customers +30%.
2
Read margin, not discount. Discount fell to 10.6% while product margin fell 4.7pp to 31.2%, because November sale prices sit in the live price. Retros of £73k across 24 brands hold retro-adjusted margin at 35.5%, down 1.8pp.
3
Bon Charge was the biggest gain, and the least efficient cover. +£48.6k on £27.1k of retro - about 1.8x, well under 3x break-even (estimate). 78% existing customers. The Ultimate Human is about 2.4x over three deal weeks. Both deserve a margin-after-cover check before Black Friday.
4
Omnilux and Hatch are the cleanest growth: +£42.2k with no cover, over 80% new customers. Protect stock on both. NodPod doubled when its 3,300-unit Black Friday order landed.
5
Our Place was stock-bound; Lumie is demand-bound. Our Place jumped from 8 to 86 units when stock arrived, reaching £8.5k - a window high, still small. Lumie sold ~40 units for the second week at £3.9k with no seasonal lift. Nurosym rose 79% on new cover but is a £6k brand.
6
Supplements grew on existing customers. LMNT +15% to a window high, Pure Encapsulations, Momentous and DFH also at window highs, Thorne +5%. Nordic, Transparent Labs and Codeage fell, the latter two to window lows.
7
November 1–15: £3.56m, +9.6% YoY. New customers −4% YoY, the narrowest gap of the autumn. Black Friday is 27 November.
