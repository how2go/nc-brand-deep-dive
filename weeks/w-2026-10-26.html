Key takeaways

1
First NC rise in five weeks, but it is a WHOOP week. NC +£20.5k and +276 new customers. WHOOP alone added £28.4k. Without WHOOP, NC fell 2.2% for a fifth consecutive week, and 249 brands were down against 224 up.
2
WHOOP was not bought with price. Life MG and Peak 5.0 took units from 67 to 159, at 1.4% discount, peaking 29–31 Oct. The driver is not confirmed. If it was paid spend or placement, it is the clearest sign in two months that NC responds to tech spend - and worth repeating into Black Friday.
3
October closed at 25,060 new customers: 86% of the 29k LE9 target, −18% YoY. LE9 assumed −5% YoY. Aug 32.4k → Sep 28.7k → Oct 25.1k. November's target is 36k, 44% above October's actual. The spend step-up into Black Friday has to carry that.
4
Correction on Withings: the lift was the retro. Cover went £4.7k → £0 and NC fell 42%, units halved. Last week I said keep whatever was running; what was running was the cover. It returned roughly 2.8x in all-customer sales on the early-October base, just under 3x break-even.
5
Oura rose for the first time in four weeks; COROS fell for a third. Oura NC +22% to £10.5k on less discount and less cover, still −76% from its £44k record. COROS £22.6k → £11.0k in three weeks, MAP, so not price.
6
Four weeks: discount down, margin up, customers down - still. NC discount 14.5% → 12.9%, NC product margin 32.1% → 33.2%, new customers 6,019 → 5,787. This week NC margin rose 0.7pp on the WHOOP mix.
7
Payday Friday broke the Friday trough. 829 new customers on Fri 30 Oct after seven weeks of Friday lows. Sunday 1 Nov's 1,025 was the best day of the last four weeks. Mon–Thu NC was flat; the weekend carried it. Next Friday shows whether this was payday or a fix.
