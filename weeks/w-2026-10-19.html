NC Brand Sales Deep Dive

−3.2% WoW

w/c 12 Oct vs 19 Oct 2026 — New customer net sales by brand (UK e-com, excl. Healf Zone, HX, POS). NC £375.6k and 5,512 new customers - fourth consecutive fall, both new lows for the window. NC discount rate rose for the first time since Healf Week (12.7% → 13.2%) and NC product margin fell (33.75% → 32.54%); volume did not come back. Total sales were up 0.9% on the existing base.

The drift reversed and the NC line still fell. For four weeks the pattern was discount down, margin up, new customers down. This week discount went back up 0.5pp and NC margin gave back 1.2pp - and new customers fell another 100 to 5,512. The extra discount sat on Pure Encapsulations (11.5% → 16.1%) and Thorne (10.2% → 12.9%) and bought existing-customer volume (+2.4%), not acquisition. October 1–25 is 20,299 new customers against 24,973 in the same days last year (−19%). Price is not the NC lever right now.
