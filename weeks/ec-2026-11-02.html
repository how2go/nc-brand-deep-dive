EC Brand Sales Deep Dive

+0.7% WoW

w/c 26 Oct vs 2 Nov 2026 — Total e-commerce net sales, all customers (UK, excl. Healf Zone, HX, POS). £1.55m, flat on last week's payday week and the highest in the window. New customers +7.5%, existing −1.7%. Discount 13.6% and product margin 35.9% are both the worst in the window. 283 of 560 brands up.

Same sales, different mix, lower margin. The existing base gave back the end-of-month payday (−£19.5k, orders −4.5%) and supplements fell with it. Gifting and devices replaced it: seven brands added £56.8k, mostly on higher discount. The Ultimate Human's deal ran a second week at 43% discount with £18.9k of cover. Discount rose 1.85pp and retro-adjusted margin fell 1.1pp to 37.25%. November 1–8 is +6.3% YoY on sales.

Key takeaways

1
£1.55m, flat on a payday week, highest in the window. New customers +7.5%, existing −1.7% with orders −4.5%. AOV up 2.5% to £57.74. Mon–Thu was +£77k; Friday and Sunday gave it back against last week's payday days.
2
Margin is at its worst in the window. Discount 13.6%, product margin 35.9%, retro-adjusted 37.25%. The Ultimate Human is about half of this week's discount rise; without it the rate rose about 1pp (estimate). Over four weeks: sales +11%, discount +3.7pp, product margin −2.1pp.
3
The Ultimate Human is now the #4 brand: £61.8k at 43% discount with £18.9k of cover. 79% existing customers. Two deal weeks returned roughly 2.5x their £29.4k of cover in extra sales, under the 3x break-even (estimate). Worth a margin-after-cover check before Black Friday.
4
Gifting and devices added £56.8k across seven brands. Hatch (+£16.7k) and Omnilux (+£13.0k) led, both on discount roughly doubling. Lola and Therabody hit window highs without extra discount. Our Place sold eight units.
5
Supplements gave back the payday week. Thorne −£8.7k, all existing customers. LMNT, Transparent Labs and MaryRuth's at window lows. Nordic, Codeage, Blueprint and DFH at window highs - DFH +17% on discount doubling to 19%.
6
Cover off, sales down - again. SURI −61%, Freja −53% and Oura −19% the week their cover ended. Withings is still falling two weeks after its cover ended, now at a window low. WHOOP gave back its spike (−£29.1k).
7
November 1–8: £1.81m, +6.3% YoY. New customers −8% YoY, against −18% for October, so the gap is narrowing. The existing base is still carrying the YoY growth.
