NC Brand Sales Deep Dive

−7.7% WoW

w/c 5 Oct vs 12 Oct 2026 — New customer net sales by brand (UK e-com, excl. Healf Zone, HX, POS). NC £387.9k and 5,612 new customers - both new lows for the window. NC discount rate 12.7%, the lowest in the window; NC product margin 33.75%, the highest. Wearables are 89% of the decline. October is running 20% below last October on new customers.

Four weeks of the same trade. Since Healf Week closed we have cut the NC discount rate every week - 18.2% → 15.6% → 14.5% → 12.7% - and NC product margin has risen with it. New customers have gone 6,036 → 6,100 → 6,019 → 5,612. October 1–18 is 14,787 new customers against 18,577 in the same days last year (−20%). Total sales are still +7% YoY, so the book is fine - but we are acquiring a fifth fewer people than a year ago, at the best margin we have had.

Key takeaways

1
October is tracking 20% below last October on new customers. 1–18 Oct: 14,787 vs 18,577 a year ago, and 17,943 in the same days of September. NC sales −16% YoY while total sales are +7%. This week's 5,612 is a new low. Month on month it is now Aug 32.4k → Sep 28.7k → Oct tracking ~25.5k. The acquisition engine has been shrinking for three months while the P&L has not noticed.
2
Four weeks of the same trade: 5.5pp of discount out, 0.8pp of margin in, 424 weekly new customers gone. NC discount 18.2% → 12.7%, NC product margin 32.9% → 33.75%, new customers 6,036 → 5,612. Every week since Healf Week has moved the same direction. It may be the right trade for Q4 margin - but it is happening by drift, and it should be a decision. Worth putting the number in front of Lestat: roughly 400 new customers a week for 0.8pp.
3
Wearables were 89% of this week's decline, and it was not price. WHOOP −£8.4k, COROS −£7.8k, Hyperice −£5.8k, Garmin −£4.0k, Oura −£2.8k. Three of them had risen for three straight weeks and all broke together; two are MAP and cannot be discounted. Strip wearables out and the book was flat. Check paid tech spend and placement for w/c 12 Oct before reading this as demand.
4
Oura needs an owner now. £44.1k → £12.6k in seven weeks, −71%, five falls in six. I said watch two weeks ago; it has fallen another 35% since. Cover and discount have both been tried and neither has held it. D2C price check and launch-cycle check are the first two moves. It was the largest NC brand in August.
5
Withings doubled in the week every other wearable fell. £6.8k → £13.6k NC, units 55 → 108, discount down 3.5pp, first £369 of cover. Into the top five for the first time. Whatever ran on Withings - creator, CRM, placement - is the single most useful thing to understand this week, because it is the only wearable that moved the right way.
6
Q4 gifting is split: five brands building, three stock-bound. Our Place four weeks up to £5.5k, Hatch above £6k, Omnilux and Therabody at window highs, NodPod back. Lola (15 units), Caraway (£353, discount cut to 1.8%) and Organic Protein Company (week 12) are not. Seven weeks to Black Friday.
7
The Friday trough is six weeks old. Friday 16 Oct's 641 new customers is the lowest day in the window. Mon 906 → Fri 641 is a 29% intra-week drop, and the shape has repeated every week since Healf Week. That is a pacing pattern for Leo, not noise.
