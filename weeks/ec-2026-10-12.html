Key takeaways

1
Lowest sales week in the window, highest margin. £1.40m, −7.2%, both segments down 7%. Discount rate 9.9% and product margin 37.99% are window records. Over four weeks: discount −2.8pp, margin +0.9pp, sales −12%, orders −7%, AOV −5%. The trade is clear and it has been running for a month. It needs to be a decision, not a drift.
2
October: sales +7% YoY, new customers −20% YoY. 1–18 Oct delivered 14,787 new customers against 18,577 in the same days last year and 17,943 in September. Month on month: Aug 32.4k → Sep 28.7k → Oct tracking ~25.5k. The P&L is growing on the existing base while the acquisition engine shrinks. That is sustainable for a quarter, not for a year.
3
Wearables −£37.8k, 35% of the decline, second time in six weeks. WHOOP, COROS and Hyperice had risen three weeks running and all broke together. COROS and Garmin are MAP - price cannot explain them. Oura is at a window low, −70% from £58.6k, with cover and discount both tried. Check paid tech spend for w/c 12 Oct; Oura needs an owner regardless.
4
Withings +91% in the week everything else in tech fell. £8.7k → £16.7k, units doubled, discount down, £369 of first cover. The single most useful thing to understand this week is what ran on Withings.
5
The supplements book is soft underneath. Pure Encapsulations, Momentous, Nordic Naturals, BodyHealth, Kiki, Biocare, Bare Biology all down. Momentous and BodyHealth have fallen four weeks running. Thorne and LMNT are holding; Form Nutrition and Transparent Labs are the only large supplement brands growing. The 28 Sep payday week flattered this group for one week.
6
Q4 gifting: five building, three stock-bound. Our Place, Hatch, Omnilux, NodPod, HigherDOSE all up. Lola (15 units, fourth week swinging), Caraway (−74% NC, discount cut to 1.8%) and Organic Protein Company (week 12) are not. Seven weeks to Black Friday.
7
The Friday trough is six weeks old. Friday 16 Oct: £174.5k and 641 new customers, both window lows. Mon-to-Fri drop is 24% on sales and 29% on new customers. Same shape every week since Healf Week. Pacing question for Leo.
