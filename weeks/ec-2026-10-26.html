Key takeaways

1
£1.54m, +9.1%, the best week since the 28 Sep payday week. Existing +10.4%, new +5.4%, orders +9.2%. End-of-month payday did a lot of it: Friday +£60.7k and Sunday £263k.
2
Two brands were 44% of the gain, and they worked in opposite ways. WHOOP +£31.0k was 89% new customers at 1.4% discount. The Ultimate Human +£25.4k was a 36–46% code deal with £12.4k of cover, 81% bought by existing customers. Without the two, the book was +5.3%.
3
Discount up 1.2pp, product margin flat, retro-adjusted margin up. 10.6% → 11.75% discount, product margin 37.3% unchanged, retro-adjusted 38.1% → 38.4% on £17.6k of retros, 70% of it The Ultimate Human. Brand funding is now carrying the extra discount.
4
Supplements rose broadly: 18 of the top 20 up. Thorne +£10.9k all from existing customers on less discount. LMNT back to £70k. Nordic and DFH ended four-week declines - DFH +10%, NC +39%. BodyHealth, Kiki, Metagenics, H2Tab and NOW at four-week highs.
5
Retro on, sales up; retro off, sales down - within one week. Withings lost £4.7k of cover and fell 46%; Hunter & Gather lost £1.5k and fell 39%. Withings returned roughly 2.8x while covered, just under the 3x break-even. Last week's "replicate Withings" was the retro.
6
October: £6.45m, +7.4% YoY, −8.2% vs September. NC sales −13.9% YoY, new customers 25,060, −18.2% and 86% of the 29k LE9 target. All of the YoY growth came from existing customers. Four-week view: existing +5%, new −6%.
7
Q4 gifting: Hatch up, Our Place stock-bound. Hatch +30% to a window high. Our Place halved on units 53 → 24 with discount up, which looks like stock. Lola flat at 40 units. HigherDOSE tripled on existing customers. Organic Protein Company in week fourteen near zero. 3.5 weeks to Black Friday.
