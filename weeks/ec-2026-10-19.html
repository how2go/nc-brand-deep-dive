Key takeaways

1
First up week in five, and it was the payday weekend on the existing base. £1.41m, +0.9%, orders flat. Existing customers +2.4%, new −3.2%. Saturday and Sunday were +£60k on the prior week; Monday to Thursday were −£54k. Against w/c 5 Oct the book is still −6%. Stabilised, not recovered.
2
The margin drift reversed: discount 9.9% → 10.6%, product margin 38.0% → 37.2%. First week since Healf Week that discount rose. Pure Encapsulations (11.5% → 16.1%) and Thorne (10.2% → 12.9%) carried it and it bought existing-customer volume. New customers fell anyway. Over four weeks discount and margin are now both flat - the gain from the two middle weeks has been handed back for one week of +0.9%.
3
October 1–25: sales +8% YoY, new customers −19% YoY, fourth consecutive weekly fall. 20,299 new customers against 24,973 last year and 23,989 in September; tracking ~25.2k for the month. The week itself ran about −14% YoY, better than the −20% of the first eighteen days, so the gap closed slightly. The growth is still all existing base.
4
Wearables are net flat but WHOOP, Oura and COROS are two weeks into a slide: −£11.7k. WHOOP and COROS are −43 to −45% from their 5 Oct highs; Oura is at a window low with £2.3k of cover. Withings (+£5.3k, window high, £3.5k cover), Hyperice and Garmin recovered. The split points at the three brands, not the channel. If paid tech spend was held both weeks, this is competitive or demand on the biggest wearable brands.
5
Supplements moved with price this week. Discount up: Pure Enc +£6k. Discount down 2–3pp: LMNT −£5.6k (window low), Form −£4.4k (first fall in five), TUH −£3.0k (cover halved). Momentous and BodyHealth broke four-week declines. Nordic Naturals is in its fourth fall and at a window low. IM8 has halved in two weeks.
6
Q4 gifting stock landed: Lola +£5.3k to a window high on 39 units, NodPod doubled, Therabody window high, Caraway off the floor. Omnilux (−42%) and HigherDOSE (−64%) gave back last week's spikes in the same week - one-week feature by the shape of it. Our Place fell for the first time in five weeks on a discount cut to 1.9%. Organic Protein Company week thirteen. A month to Black Friday.
7
Retros £10.6k, +30%, and they are converting unevenly. Withings +£1.7k of cover for +£5.3k of sales. Hunter & Gather first £1.7k for +£3.4k, mostly existing. Oura £2.3k for −£3.1k. The Ultimate Human halved its cover and fell 17%. w/c 12 Oct retros restated from £4.4k to £8.2k - last week's retro-adjusted margin was understated by 0.3pp.
8
Friday is the low day for the seventh week, but it beat the prior Friday for the first time since Healf Week. 648 new customers and £180.5k against 641 and £174.5k. Mon-to-Fri drop 23% on new customers. Sunday £236k was the largest day of the fortnight. Pacing question for Leo stands.
